HealthSync – AI-Powered Health Monitoring and Assistance Platform
Project Overview

HealthSync is an intelligent health monitoring web application designed to help users track, analyze, and improve their daily health habits through a personalized and interactive experience. The platform combines health data tracking with an AI-inspired Health Assistant chatbot that provides instant feedback, recommendations, and wellness guidance based on user inputs.

The system enables users to monitor important lifestyle parameters such as calorie consumption, water intake, sleep duration, and physical activity. By analyzing these metrics, HealthSync calculates key health indicators including BMI (Body Mass Index), BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate), daily calorie requirements, recommended water intake, and an overall Health Score.

A major highlight of the platform is its Health Assistant Chatbot, which acts as a virtual wellness companion by answering health-related questions, interpreting user data, and providing personalized suggestions to encourage healthier lifestyle choices.

Problem Statement

Many individuals struggle to maintain healthy habits due to busy schedules, lack of health awareness, and fragmented health information spread across multiple applications. While health-tracking tools exist, many fail to provide meaningful insights or interactive guidance.

HealthSync addresses this challenge by integrating health tracking, data visualization, personalized recommendations, and conversational assistance into a single platform that is easy to use and accessible to a broad range of users.
